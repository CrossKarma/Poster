Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

Welcome to Poster. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and otherwise process the personal information of users of the Poster web application (the "Service"). Poster is operated by an independent developer ("we", "us", or "our").

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide when signing in. When you sign in with your Google/YouTube account, we receive your email address, your Google account identifier, and the name of your YouTube channel. We also receive and store a refresh token that allows the Service to access your YouTube channel and upload videos to it at your direction.

Content you submit. When you use the Service to generate titles, descriptions, hashtags, and captions, still frames sampled from your video and the video's file name are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to produce that text. Your video file itself is never uploaded to or stored on our servers; it is uploaded directly from your browser to YouTube. We also store the content niche you enter in your settings.

YouTube channel and video data. With your permission, the Service may collect data about your YouTube channel and the videos on it, including video metadata such as titles, descriptions, tags, upload dates, durations, view counts, likes, comments, and audience and performance analytics made available through the YouTube API. We use this data to understand how your content performs.

Trending and public content data. The Service may collect publicly available information about trending topics, popular videos, and commonly used hashtags in order to inform its recommendations.

Usage information. We keep a count of how many generations your account has run, your account creation date, and your trial or subscription status.

Payment information. If you subscribe, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card number. We store a Stripe customer identifier and your subscription status so the Service knows your account is active.

We do not collect your date of birth, phone number, physical address, precise location, or advertising identifiers. We do not use advertising trackers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate the Service: to sign you in, to generate listing copy for your videos, to upload videos to your own YouTube channel when you ask us to, to manage your free trial and subscription, and to respond to support requests.

We also use your channel data, video metadata, performance analytics, and trending data to provide personalized recommendations, such as suggested titles, descriptions, hashtags, and posting times intended to improve how your content performs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How We Share Your Information

Google / YouTube. The Service uses YouTube API Services. By using the Service you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Poster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke Poster's access to your Google account at any time on Google's security settings page. For more information about Google's privacy practices, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Anthropic. Video frames, file names, and content you submit for generation, along with relevant channel and trend context, are processed by Anthropic's Claude API to produce your titles, descriptions, captions, and recommendations.

Stripe. If you subscribe, Stripe processes your payment. See the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Hosting. The Service and its database are hosted on Render.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Service and its users. We do not share your information with any other third parties, and we do not sell or "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under applicable law.

4. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and the right to appeal a decision about your request. You may exercise these rights by emailing us at the address in the Contact section below. We will verify your request by asking you to send it from the email address associated with your account. We will not retaliate against you for exercising your rights.

You can disconnect your YouTube account at any time by revoking Poster's access on Google's security settings page. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete your stored information, including your refresh token and any stored channel data.

5. Data Security

We maintain technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or loss. All traffic to the Service is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Refresh tokens are stored in our database and are never exposed to other users or third parties beyond the Google token endpoint.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account exists. If you request deletion of your account, we delete your stored personal information. We may retain limited records where necessary to comply with legal obligations or to resolve disputes.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at and is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Google does not permit children under 13 to hold standard Google accounts, and the Service requires a Google sign-in. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

9. Contact Us
